## Case-specific interviewer note

Request the economic equation before arithmetic. Under the publisher inputs, contribution is $0.10 per unit; $40 million fixed cost therefore requires 400 million units. At eight units per gallon, that is 50 million gallons, or 12.5% of a 400-million-gallon market. Keep units explicit and return to the feasibility of winning that share.
