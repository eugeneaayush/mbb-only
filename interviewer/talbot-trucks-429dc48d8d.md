## Case-specific interviewer note

Ask for a total-cost-of-ownership structure before requesting a vehicle price. Under the case assumptions, diesel annual cost is $106,000 and electric cost before depreciation is $59,000. The $47,000 annual headroom over four years supports a $188,000 price in the simplified model. Preserve the source's lifetime and depreciation assumptions; this is not an independent discounted-cash-flow valuation.
