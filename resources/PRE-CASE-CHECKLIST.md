# Before your partner joins

- Have I read the whole case, including the solution and every exhibit?
- Can I explain the objective, important constraints and the intended decision in one sentence?
- Have I recalculated the main quantitative steps with units and time periods?
- Do I know which information is available on request, which is revealed at a specific question, and which I must not disclose?
- Have I visually checked the exact candidate exhibits for answer notes, speaker notes and hidden material?
- Can I recognize at least one sound alternative approach to the author's proposed structure?
- Are we doing a coaching rep, an interview simulation or a benchmark? Have we agreed how hints work?
- Is the case new to my partner? Exposure changes how we interpret the result.
- Have I left time for evidence-based feedback and a redo?

Do not give a case you have only skimmed. If you discover an inconsistent answer, preserve the source's facts, identify the inconsistency and discuss the smallest explicit assumption needed to resolve it. Do not silently invent missing data.
