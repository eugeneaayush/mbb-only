### Readiness gate

- [ ] Prompt and all necessary inputs are present.
- [ ] Exhibits are legible and free of solution annotations.
- [ ] Every required calculation independently checked.
- [ ] All source gaps and invented assumptions labelled.
- [ ] A peer has given the case once and defects have been corrected.
- [ ] Candidate packet and interviewer key are separated.
- [ ] Provenance and public sharing basis recorded.
