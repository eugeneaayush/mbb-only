# Peer case scorecard

Original practice rubric; scores are observations, not a firm's hiring threshold. Record the case ID, edition, date, mode and prior exposure in your private log.

For every scored dimension, record an observed example or calculation, the effect on the case, and the assistance level (0 none; 1 nudge; 2 direction; 3 worked step). Use **NS** when the case did not test a dimension. A different valid framework is not an error.

| Dimension | 1: substantial support | 3: functional, inconsistent | 5: independent and convincing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | No usable decomposition or decision logic | Relevant branches, but priorities or links to the objective are weak | Tailored decision tree; explains priorities and adapts to evidence |
| Quantitative analysis | Cannot set up or interpret required calculation | Mostly correct setup; slips, units or checks need prompting | Clear formula, accurate arithmetic, units, sanity check and business implication |
| Exhibits | Misses units or central comparison | Reads data correctly but implication is shallow | Quantifies the useful pattern, separates observation from hypothesis, chooses the next test |
| Business judgement | Unsupported conclusion or ignored constraints | Defensible idea with limited trade-off analysis | Weighs alternatives, economics, feasibility and the uncertainties that could change the answer |
| Communication | Reasoning is hard to follow | Understandable but some transitions or explanations missing | Explains intent, uses concise signposts, listens and stays composed; pauses purposefully |
| Synthesis | No clear answer to the client | Recommendation with some evidence; risks or next step generic | Decision first, prioritized evidence, specific risks and practical next action |

Use 2 or 4 when performance falls between adjacent anchors; do not average away a serious gap. There is no official pass score.
